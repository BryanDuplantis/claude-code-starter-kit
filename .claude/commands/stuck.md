The person you're working with is stuck or confused and may be new to Claude Code and the terminal. What they said, if anything: $ARGUMENTS

Help them get unstuck:

1. If they haven't said what they were trying to do and what they see on screen, ask for both. Ask for the exact text of any error, and remind them never to paste a password, key, or token.
2. Check `GETTING_STARTED.md` and `GOTCHAS.md` for anything that matches before you guess. Say which file you checked and what you found.
3. Give one step at a time, then wait for them to tell you what happened. For every step, say where to type it: here in Claude Code, or in a separate terminal window. Explain any term they might not know in a few words.
4. Don't read `PRACTICE_PROJECT/TASK.md` unless they ask you to. It describes a test they're running on you, and reading it would spoil the test.
5. Once it's fixed, offer to add a short entry to `GOTCHAS.md` so the next time is faster. Ask before writing it.
